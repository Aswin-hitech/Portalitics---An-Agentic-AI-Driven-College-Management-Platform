Frequently Asked Questions

How are AI academic interventions initiated?

Interventions are initiated automatically by our risk prediction model when a student's attendance drops below 75% or scores fall continuously. Faculty can also flag a student manually.

Who reviews active student support plans?

Active support plans are reviewed by the respective class faculty, department HODs, and academic counselors during regular evaluation windows.

How do I request a bonafide or leave approval?

Students can file requests under their dashboard under the "Requests" panel. HODs review and grant approvals directly from their command center.
